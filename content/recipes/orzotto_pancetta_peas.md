---
title: Orzotto with pancetta & peas
tags: [dinner]
---

## Ingredients

- 100g diced pancetta
- 1 small onion, finely chopped
- 1 garlic clove, crushed
- 125g orzo pasta
- 75ml white wine (optional)
- 400ml hot chicken stock
- 50g frozen peas
- 25g butter
- 25g parmesan, grated
- Salt and black pepper

## Method

- Fry pancetta in large pan until crispy
- Add onion, cook until soft
- Add garlic and orzo, stir for 1 minute
- Add wine if using, let bubble
- Add stock gradually, stirring frequently like risotto, about 15 minutes
- Stir in peas for last 3 minutes
- Remove from heat, stir in butter and parmesan
- Season and serve
